Privacy

Sanathani · last updated 2 November 2026

Sanathani is a Panchangam, family-reminder and pooja app. It works without an account, and most of what you enter never leaves your phone.

What stays on your device

Family events, birth details, horoscope details, your own songs and poojas, japa counts, deeksha progress and settings are saved in the app on your device. If you use “Use my location” to pick your town, the location is used on the device to find the nearest place and is not sent to us.

If you sign in

Signing in is optional. If you do, we keep your email address and a copy of the items above so they can sync between your devices. They are stored with our database provider, Supabase, and only you can read them. You can delete your account and its copy at any time in Settings → Account.

Deeksha groups

If you join a deeksha group, your display name, the group's events and the messages you send are visible to the members of that group. Groups listed as “nearby” show only a rough area name that the group chose, never an exact location. You can block and report other members.

Ads

The website may show a small Google ad on the Home and Calendar screens only, never on stotrams, songs, pooja steps, japa or deeksha screens. The ads are not personalized. Google may use cookies to show and measure them and to prevent fraud; see how Google uses information from sites that use its services. We block adult, dating, gambling, alcohol and other categories that do not suit a devotional app.

What we never do

We do not sell your data, and we do not share your family details, birth details or messages with advertisers.

Children

Sanathani is a family app but is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
